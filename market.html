Decision rules

  • Send only where the margin clears your minimum. If none does, buy less today or sell locally.
  • If the top two markets are within ₹75/qtl, choose the nearer or faster-paying one, or split trucks.
  • Keep any one market under about 40% of a week's trucks.
  • Agmarknet prices are often a day old and some mandis skip days — check today's rate with your agent before loading.
Prices: Agmarknet via data.gov.in. Distances, freight, commission and weight-loss defaults are rough starting values. Decision aid, not financial advice.
